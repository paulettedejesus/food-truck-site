## What changes at each breakpoint

- Phones: Boxes and Cards are stacked on top of one another headings are also in alignment with boxes and cards everything is in one column
- From md (768px): Cards and boxes are side by side two columns headings are increased to match the medium layout 
- From lg (992px): Cards are in 2 rows and are side by side with 3 columns, the boxes are in  are also side by side and headings have a bigger font-size that matches with the large layout increases in size when I move it from smaller to larger
